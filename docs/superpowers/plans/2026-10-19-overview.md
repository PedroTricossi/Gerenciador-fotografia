# Business Overview Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add Resumo and Extrato tabs to the Negócio page. Together they show what came in, what went out, what's free today and what's coming in the next months. Add a transport price guide built from Pedro's recorded trips.

**Architecture:**
- **Backend:** read-only.
  - A pure `domain/overview.py` holds the period, statement, upcoming-months, palette-slot and trip-guide rules.
  - An `OverviewService` reads the instalments table and reuses `ExpenseEntryService` (given an uncapped date-range method), so Resumo, Extrato and Despesas agree by construction.
  - Two GET endpoints: `/overview` and `/transport-guide`.
  - No migration, no audit.
- **Frontend:**
  - two new tabs, Resumo and Extrato, built from `ui/`;
  - a shared period control;
  - a validated categorical palette as `ui/` tokens.

**Tech Stack:**
- Backend: Python 3.13, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy 2.0, pytest.
- Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Query, Vitest, MSW.
- End to end: Playwright with axe.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-overview-design.md`

## Global Constraints

**Counting rules**
- **Money in:** a paid instalment counts on its `paid_on` date (cash basis), for its `amount_cents`.
  - Only instalments of non-deleted contracts count, except that a **paid** instalment of a deleted contract still counts. It shows with `contract_deleted=true` and null names.
  - Unpaid instalments of deleted contracts never count.
- **Money out:** every entry `ExpenseEntryService` produces counts on its `date`. Entries with `missing_amount` count as 0.
- **Up to today:** period totals, the bar, the statement and `free_today` count only items dated **≤ today**.
- **Today:** the client sends `today=YYYY-MM-DD`, which is the browser's local date. The server only validates the format.
- **Period:** `period=month&value=YYYY-MM` or `period=year&value=YYYY`.
  - If the period's first day is after today, the response says `not_started=true`, with zero totals and an empty statement.
- **Per-type grouping:** by `type_id`.

**Palette**
- A type's `palette_slot` is its index in all expense types ordered by `(created_at, id)`, archived types included.
- Slots 0–7 are real colours. A slot of 8 or more becomes `null`, and those types merge into one "Outros" slice.

**Próximos meses**
- **First row:** the current month (`rest_of_month=true`). Its in-total is unpaid instalments with `month == current` plus paid instalments with `paid_on > today` in this month. Its out-total is entries dated `> today` in this month.
- **Then one row per month:** for `max(3, months up to the last month holding an unpaid instalment or a future paid_on)` months after the current one, capped at 24.
  - The in-total is unpaid instalments with that `month`, plus paid instalments whose `paid_on` falls in it.
  - The out-total is all entries dated in that month.
- **Projection:** `projected_free` starts from `free_today` and adds in − out row by row.
- **Late:** `late_cents` is unpaid instalments with `month <` current month, on non-deleted contracts. They are never in any row.

**Trip guide**
- **One trip:** the transport-type manual expenses (`type_key="transport"`) linked to one `(contract_id, event_kind)`, dated ≤ today.
  - `cost` is the sum of those legs.
  - `distance_tenths` is the max non-null distance among them. A trip without one is excluded.
  - `left_home_at` is the earliest non-null time among them.
  - `transport_kind` belongs to the leg with the earliest departure, or the first leg by date when no leg has a time.
- **Similar trips:** `|d − D| ≤ max(150, ceil(D × 0.25))` tenths. The typical cost is the median, and min/max are given, only when there are ≥ 3 similar trips.
- **Per kind:** the median of `cost / km` over all trips of that kind. Shown only with ≥ 3 trips.
- **Night:** departure ≥ 18:00. Day/night medians of `cost / km` are shown only when both sides have ≥ 3 trips.
- **Median:** the mean of the two middle values when the count is even, rounded half-up to whole centavos.

**Portuguese UI text**
- **Tabs and controls:**
  - "Parcelas", "Despesas", "Resumo", "Extrato" (group "Área"); `?aba=resumo|extrato`.
  - "Mês", "Ano" (group "Período").
- **Resumo:**
  - "Entrou", "Saiu", "Livre no mês" / "Livre no ano", "Livre hoje".
  - Bar heading: "Para onde foi o que entrou". Slice "Livre", and "Outros" for slot `null`.
  - "Saiu mais do que entrou neste período: R$ x a mais" and "Nada entrou ou saiu neste período."
  - "Este período ainda não começou — veja Próximos meses."
  - "Há N casamento(s) com 2º fotógrafo sem valor definido".
- **Próximos meses:**

  - "Parcelas atrasadas não contadas: R$ x".

**Project rules**
- All existing rules hold:
  - layered backend, RFC 9457 problems, integer centavos, months `YYYY-MM`, never a method named `list`;
  - `ui/` tokens only and feature CSS for layout only;
  - WCAG 2.2 AA, CSP `default-src 'self'`, no chart library;
  - tests query by role, label and text;
  - no edits to pre-existing tests unless a task names them.
- **The bar:** plain HTML/CSS with a 2px surface gap between slices and an accessible name ("Para onde foi o que entrou"). The legend lists every slice with a swatch, name, `%` to one decimal and amount. Slice order is largest first, Livre last.

## Review Focus

1. **An instalment due in September but paid in October, and one marked paid with a future date:** the first counts in October Entrou and isn't late. The second is absent from Livre hoje but appears in Próximos meses in its `paid_on` month. Tests in Tasks 1 and 2.
2. **Despesas, Resumo and Extrato disagreeing about one month:** for any month up to today, Resumo's Saiu equals the sum of `/expense-entries` entries dated ≤ today. Extrato's final running balance equals `free_today` when the period is the current one. Test in Task 2.
3. **A month where Saiu > Entrou, or Entrou = 0:** no `NaN%`, no bar over 100%, and the warning text shows. Tests in Tasks 1 and 5.
4. **Year boundaries:** December → January in the month arrows and in Próximos meses, and the year view of a year in progress. Tests in Tasks 1 and 4.
5. **Two legs of one wedding (ida and volta), one without a distance:** they count as one trip with summed cost and max distance. A trip whose only leg has no distance is excluded. Test in Task 1.
